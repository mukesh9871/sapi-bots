Pack 3 (Milestone 9). Four starter routines (P-ROUT-2). They are installed switched off:
open **Schedules**, check the time and the task, and switch on the ones you want.

| Routine | Who does it | When (change it as you like) |
|---|---|---|
| Morning briefing | Briefer: searches the web for the topics in its task | every day at 07:30, tells you when done |
| Weekly summary | Reporter: reads the hub's activity log and schedule runs | Mondays at 09:00 |
| Stretch reminder | Reminder: writes a short, friendly reminder | every day at 15:00, tells you |
| Tidy my folder | Tidier: sorts files into subfolders by type | Fridays at 16:00 |

- **Tidy my folder** needs a folder: set it on the Packs page (Settings). The tidier sees
  only that folder, and every move goes through Approvals (installed as Ask).
- To make your own routine, open Schedules and describe it in plain words: the hub drafts
  it (who, what, when) and you check it before saving.
