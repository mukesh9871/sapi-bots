# Settings you fill in on the Packs page: "<key> | folder|text|longtext|secret | <label>"
linkedin_niche | longtext | LinkedIn: niche (what you post about)
linkedin_voice | longtext | LinkedIn: voice (how you sound)
linkedin_audience | longtext | LinkedIn: audience (who you write for)
linkedin_avoid | text | LinkedIn: topics never to post about (comma-separated, matched as whole words)
linkedin_announce_domains | text | LinkedIn: sites your announcements may link to (comma-separated, for example youtube.com)
linkedin_author | text | LinkedIn: your member URN (urn:li:person:...)
linkedin_token | secret | LinkedIn: access token with w_member_social (kept in Windows Credential Manager)
